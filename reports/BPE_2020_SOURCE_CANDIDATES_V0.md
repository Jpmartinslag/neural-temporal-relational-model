## Current Mosaicking Assessment

- Full national mosaic is not feasible from discovered public endpoints yet.
- A partial mosaic would be scientifically weak because it would overrepresent one department (`76`) and one blocked regional candidate (`Occitanie`) rather than France mainland coverage.
- For the project pipeline, BPE 2020 should remain a documented gap unless a user-supplied archive or a new official mirror is found.
